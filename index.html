Data Sources & Methodology

World Bank Open Data

Primary data source providing comprehensive economic indicators for 217 countries. The World Bank Open Data platform offers free access to development data from around the world, including economic, social, and environmental indicators.

Key Indicators

GDP (current US$): Gross domestic product at current market prices in US dollars. This indicator represents the total monetary value of all finished goods and services produced within a country.

Population, total: Total population based on the de facto definition of population, which counts all residents regardless of legal status or citizenship.

Data Processing

Data extracted using the World Bank API for the period 2015-2024. Countries are filtered to exclude regional aggregates and focus on individual nation-states. Visualizations created using Plotly for interactive exploration.
